Windows 11

Traps this board sets

BIOS before CPU, always

Flash to 5862 or newer while the Ryzen 1400 is still in the socket. A 2017 BIOS won’t POST with a Zen 3 chip, and you need a working CPU to run the update.

Two slots means replace, not add

Both DIMM slots are already full with 8 GB sticks. Going to 32 GB means the GeIL pair comes out. There is no adding a stick, and 32 GB is the board’s hard ceiling.

The M.2 hides under the graphics card

Pull the GPU to reach M.2_1, then put it back. Ten extra minutes, no extra parts.

B550 will not start with your CPU

B550 and X570 officially begin at Ryzen 3000. Your 1400 is Zen 1, so those boards force a CPU purchase in the same order. A B450 takes everything from the 1400 to a 5700X — drop it in today, upgrade the chip whenever.

Four slots changes the RAM math

With four DIMMs you keep the GeIL pair and add two sticks rather than replacing both. In a market where a 2 × 16 kit runs $220, that is the difference between $115 and $220 for the same 32 GB.

PCIe 3.0 taxes new cards

A 9060 XT or 5060 Ti uses a PCIe 5.0 ×8 link, which drops to 3.0 ×8 here. Used 6700 XT and 3060 cards are 4.0 ×16 and lose nothing.
